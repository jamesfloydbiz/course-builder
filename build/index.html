Course Builder

Upload your standards, study guide or syllabus and set your test date. You get a course built backward from what the test needs: lessons in small steps and a large bank of practice questions. When you start studying, a short placement test finds what you already know, and the plan fits the days you have left.

Free. Every course you build is saved to your account and, unless you switch it off below, shared in the public library so others can use it. The file you upload is never shared, only the lessons and questions built from it. Building takes about 10 to 15 minutes, and you need to keep this tab open the whole time.

Don't close or refresh this tab while it builds. You'll get a chance to review the plan before the long part starts.
